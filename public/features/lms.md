# AXON LMS (Learning Management System)
The LMS connects teachers and students for seamless academic execution outside the classroom.

## Core Features
*   **Assignments:** Teachers can create tasks with titles, descriptions, due dates, and maximum marks. Settings include toggles for late submissions and group tasks.
*   **Study Materials:** A centralized repository for uploading PDFs, attaching URLs, and managing class-wise resource folders.
*   **Student Submissions:** Students can view due dates, upload submission files, and add comments directly to their teachers.
*   **Evaluation Dashboard:** Teachers can track overall submission rates, view pending evaluations, and assign grades that sync with the student's profile.
