# AXON Communication & SETU
A unified communication layer designed for school-wide broadcasts and targeted messaging.

## Core Features
*   **SETU Real-Time Chat:** A secure messaging platform connecting Teachers ↔ Parents, Admins ↔ Teachers, and internal Staff.
*   **Announcements & Circulars:** Broadcast official messages with attachments to specific audiences, such as the entire school, a specific class/section, or teachers only.
*   **Smart Notifications:** System-generated alerts with read/unread tracking for overdue payments, unresolved tickets, low attendance, or subscription expirations.
