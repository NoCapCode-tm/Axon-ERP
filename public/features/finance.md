# AXON Finance & Fee Management
The finance module handles fee structures, installments, and payment tracking across the school.

## Core Features
*   **Fee Structures:** Create customized fee rules mapped to academic years and classes (e.g., Tuition, Transport, Library, Examination).
*   **Installment Setup:** Define specific due dates and breakdown amounts for easy tracking and automated reminders.
*   **Payment Tracking:** Monitor real-time student payment statuses (Paid, Partial, Pending, Overdue).
*   **Defaulter Management:** Automatically generate pending dues lists and apply late fines based on predefined rules.
*   **Financial Reports:** Super Admins and School Admins can view daily collection charts, monthly revenue growth, and plan-wise revenue.
